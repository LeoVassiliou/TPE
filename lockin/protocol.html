What actually changes by the 7th

Roughly 1.5–3kg of fullness from creatine saturation and glycogen refilled by eating properly. On a 52kg frame that genuinely changes how a t-shirt sits. You have already banked most of it — the creatine every day was the right call.

What does not change: upper chest size, triceps size, the flat marks on your arms and chest. Five days is not a small amount of muscle growth — it's zero. Anyone promising otherwise is selling something. Knowing this now is what stops the 7th feeling like a failure.

03

The block

9 → 24 August · sixteen days, no deadline

This is the one that actually works. Sixteen days with nobody to impress at the end of it is long enough for skin, teeth, sleep and habits to genuinely change — and those are most of what you're actually insecure about.

Read this before anything else

You said you want to do it perfectly. That single word has ended every previous attempt, and it will end this one too.

The mechanism is the same loop you already know: day four goes wrong, "perfect" is broken, the whole thing feels contaminated, you stop. That is "not worth it → do nothing" wearing a more ambitious costume.

So there is no "perfect" in this block. There is a floor and there is a target. Hitting the floor means the day counted. Ten target days and six floor days beats four perfect days followed by quitting, by an enormous margin — and it is the version that actually happens.

Non-negotiable · 15 min
The floor
  • Eat three times
  • 5g creatine
  • 2L water
  • Night routine — floss, brush, tongue, moisturiser

Hit these and the day counted. Not a good day — a day that happened. Nothing gets written off, ever.

A good day
The target
  • 2,900 kcal · four meals
  • Session per the rotation
  • Skincare AM + PM, shower after training
  • 30 min reading
  • 8 hours, fixed wake time

Aim here. Land here most days. Do not punish yourself for the others.

Skin — the escalation you have been waiting for

Aug 9 is the day you start the retinoid. This was wrong before the 7th and it is right now, because the purge finally has room to happen. Adapalene 0.1%, pea-sized, at night, every third night for the first week, then every other night. Moisturiser on top.

Everything else continues: BP wash on the back and chest with 60 seconds of contact, salicylic acid on the nose, SPF every morning, niacinamide if you have it.

Your face will get worse before it gets better

Around days 7–14 of the retinoid your skin will likely look worse — more spots, some flaking, some redness. This is the purge: microcomedones that were already under the surface all coming up at once. It is the drug working, not failing.

This is the moment you would normally quit. Don't. Push through and it clears from roughly week 4 onward, and the result is better skin than anything else on this page can give you. Do not score the block on how your face looks on Aug 24 — that one is a three-month investment that happens to start here.

Measurement — do it twice, not daily

  • Aug 9 and Aug 24 only: photos in the same spot, same light, same time of day, same pose — front, side, back. Plus weight, and a tape measure round the upper arm and chest if you have one.
  • Weigh daily but only ever read the 7-day average. Daily weight swings 1–2kg on food volume and hydration alone — a single reading contains almost no signal.
  • Do not check the mirror looking for change. Daily mirror checks show you nothing and make you anxious. The day-1 vs day-16 photo pair is where you will actually see it.

04

The rules

the ones that make it survive contact with a bad day
  • A late thing still counts. A small thing still counts. A 1,800 calorie day is a day that happened, not a failed one.
  • Never write off a day. 4pm with nothing done is not a lost day — it's water, food, creatine, and the day counted.
  • No streaks. A streak is a thing that breaks, and a broken streak is the excuse you've been waiting for.
  • Test, don't guess, on weights. If you get past the top of the rep range with clean form, add weight. Micro-load with the 1.25kg plates.
  • Write down every weight and rep. Without the record you can't beat last time, and beating last time is the entire mechanism.
  • Stop 2–3 reps short of failure. Failure adds fatigue faster than muscle, and fatigue is what makes you skip day 12.
  • Don't pick anything on your skin. A spot left alone lasts days. A spot you pick becomes a mark that lasts months.
  • Shower immediately after training, not an hour later. Sweat sitting on your back is the single biggest driver of what's there.
  • When it goes wrong, open the app, not the chat. The card will tell you the next thing faster than asking will.
